Overview for Google Search

Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-18

Overview is a browser extension that answers Google Search queries with cited sources, using AI providers you configure. Its author operates no server: nothing you do with the extension is sent to, logged by, stored by or monetized by the author.

Your consent

Overview sends nothing to any provider until you agree to how it works. On first use it shows a short explanation of what is sent and to whom, and asks you to choose Agree and continue. You can withdraw that consent at any time in Settings → Privacy; Overview then stops sending anything until you agree again. If this policy changes in a way that affects what is sent, Overview asks you again.

What is processed, and by whom

When Overview is on, you have agreed, and you search on www.google.com:

Data Sent to Why
Your search query, a retrieval objective and a keyword version derived from it, any domain/date filters, and the sites you chose to block Parallel AI (api.parallel.ai) with your API key To find web sources
(Deep search only) up to 5 URLs selected from those results Parallel AI To extract page excerpts
Your query and the retrieved source excerpts OpenRouter (openrouter.ai) with your API key, or your local Ollama server (127.0.0.1 / localhost) To write the cited answer
The answer's statements and the source excerpts they cite The same model provider To check each statement against its sources for the accuracy score (when the score is on; it can be turned off in Settings)
For a follow-up question: the question, plus the earlier questions and answers of that conversation (the last three) The same model provider To understand the follow-up and turn it into a search
Your OpenRouter key (key-information request only, no search data) OpenRouter To check your free-model daily allowance, which decides how many free requests a search may use
Each API key, when you choose Save and check Its own provider only To confirm the key works

When you submit a new query from Google's own search box, the web search for it may start a moment before the next page loads, so the answer appears sooner.

Requests go directly from your browser to these endpoints over HTTPS (Ollama runs on your own computer and is reached over its local address). As with any HTTPS request, providers receive normal network metadata such as your IP address. Their handling of data is governed by their own policies: OpenRouter and Parallel AI.

Search queries and questions are free text. Anything you type into them, including personal information, is sent as written.

When Show Overview is turned off in Settings, the extension only hides Google's AI Overview: it sends nothing to any provider. Spelling corrections, ads and results on Google's page are never changed.

The extension does not send your browsing history, the contents of Google's page, or any other personal data. It does not use analytics, telemetry, tracking, ads or remote code. Its author never sees your data.

What is stored, and where

Everything stays on your device unless noted.

  • API keys, preferences (including blocked sites) and your consent choice: chrome.storage.local, restricted to the extension's trusted contexts. The content script running on Google pages cannot read them. Keys are sent only to their own provider.
  • Saved conversations: the extension's own IndexedDB on this device. A search is saved only when you continue it (a follow-up, Open conversation, or Google's AI Mode tab). Conversations are never uploaded or synced. Delete one or all of them from the Conversations page at any time.
  • Overview on/off: a single on/off value in chrome.storage.sync. If you use Chrome sync, your browser syncs this one value between your devices; it contains nothing else.
  • Request state, the evidence behind the current answer, recent answers and short-lived caches: chrome.storage.session, which Chrome clears when the browser session ends.

Use Reset all settings in Settings to remove your keys, preferences and consent, and Delete all on the Conversations page to remove saved conversations. Uninstalling the extension removes all of its stored data.

Limited Use

The use of information received by Overview adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide Overview's single purpose, cited answers to your Google searches, and is sent only to the providers you configure for that purpose. It is never sold, never used for advertising, never used to determine creditworthiness or for lending, and never read by the author or any other person.

Children

Overview is not directed to children and does not knowingly process children's data.
